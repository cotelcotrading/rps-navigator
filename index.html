About the RPS Compliance Model

The RPS Compliance Model is a web-based tool that helps electric cooperatives (ECs) and distribution utilities check their position under the DOE Renewable Portfolio Standards (RPS) for on-grid areas. From an EC's recorded data for CP 2020–2025 it forecasts sales with Holt's linear exponential smoothing, computes the REC obligation each year to 2040, compares it with the RECs the EC can actually secure, and prices any shortfall at the DOE ceiling.

What it answersWill our RECs cover the obligation each year? If not, how many RECs are missing, and what could they cost?
How it computesObligation = ROUNDDOWN[prior-period GES × (Ko + Km)]. Supply = PSA, own generation/net-metering, FIT and banked RECs. 1 REC = 1 MWh.
Where it is appliedPlanning REC procurement and REM purchases, budgeting compliance cost, screening new PSAs, and testing REC price levels before the compliance period closes.

Compliance position & supply sources

Shows whether the EC's RECs cover its yearly obligation, and where those RECs come from. A year is compliant when eligible RECs plus the bank brought in cover the obligation; otherwise the gap is the REC shortfall. Years CP 2020–2025 are recorded data; CP 2026–2040 are Holt forecasts.

  • Obligation vs eligible RECs and REC bankCompares the yearly obligation with the RECs created and the RECs left in the bank.
  • How the obligation is metBreaks the supply into PSA, own generation/net-metering, FIT and bank, and shows the shortfall on top.
  • PSA contracted capacityMW contracted per source; only RE-eligible PSAs earn RECs.
  • FIT RECsActual FIT RECs and the Holt forecast for later years.

    Model parameters

    These are the gold input cells of the workbook sheets. Changes recalculate every sheet below and the dashboard.
    RPSn = ROUNDDOWN[ (ES + NM + RE-OU + GEOP)n−1 × (Ko + Km)n ]  |  TER = ROUNDDOWN(created RECs + carry-over)  |  Shortfall = MAX(0, RPS − TER − bank)  |  Cost = Shortfall × price
